# Feature 20: Attribute An Expense To Payer And Split

```gherkin
Feature: Attribute an expense to payer and split
  As a participant
  I want to record who paid for an expense and who it's split among
  So that the group's expenses are correctly attributed for later balance calculations

  Scenario: I record an expense split evenly among participants
    Given I am recording an expense of SGD 30.00 on a shared trip
    When I select myself as payer and split it evenly among 3 participants
    Then the expense is saved with me as payer and each of the 3 sharing SGD 10.00

  Scenario: Payer and split-among default to sensible values
    Given I open the expense form on a shared trip
    Then I am pre-selected as the payer
    And all current participants are pre-selected in the split

  Scenario: I split an expense by exact amounts instead of evenly
    Given I am recording an expense of SGD 30.00 on a shared trip
    When I choose to split it by exact amounts and enter each participant's share
    Then the expense is saved with each participant's entered exact share

  Scenario: An uneven equal split rounds correctly
    Given I am recording an expense of SGD 10.00
    When I split it evenly among 3 participants
    Then the shares are shown rounded to 2 decimal places
    And the shares sum to exactly SGD 10.00

  Scenario: Exact amounts that don't add up to the total are refused
    Given I am splitting an expense of SGD 30.00 by exact amounts
    When the amounts I enter do not sum to SGD 30.00
    Then I see an error and the expense is not saved

  Scenario: Saving with no one selected in the split is refused
    Given I am recording an expense on a shared trip
    When I deselect every participant from the split, including the payer
    Then I see an error and the expense is not saved

  Scenario: Switching split method recalculates the shares
    Given I have entered exact amounts for a split
    When I switch back to an even split
    Then the shares are recalculated evenly, not left as my exact entries

  Scenario: A departed participant's name still appears on past attributions
    Given a past expense was paid by or split with a participant who has since left or been removed
    When I view that expense
    Then it still shows that participant's name as payer or in the split

  Scenario: I edit an existing expense's payer or split
    Given I am viewing a previously recorded shared-trip expense
    When I change who paid or who it's split among
    Then the updated attribution is saved

  Scenario: A solo trip's expense form has no payer or split fields
    Given I am recording an expense on my own solo (unshared) trip
    Then I do not see any payer or split-among fields

  Scenario: Saving fails when the server is unreachable
    Given I am recording an expense on a shared trip
    When I submit it and the app cannot reach the server
    Then I see a friendly error message
    And no expense record is created
```

## Assumptions

1. Two split methods are supported: an even split across selected participants, and an exact
   amount typed per participant. Percentage-based splitting is not supported in v2.
2. Payer and split-among fields only appear on the expense form for shared trips; a solo
   (unshared) trip's expense form is unchanged from feature 005.
3. The payer defaults to the person recording the expense; the split-among selection defaults to
   every current participant, both changeable before saving.
4. An even split's per-person shares are rounded to 2 decimal places and always sum to exactly the
   expense total (any rounding remainder is absorbed into one share rather than lost).
5. An exact-amount split must have its entered shares sum exactly to the expense total, or the
   form refuses to save.
6. At least one participant (which may be only the payer) must remain selected in the split; an
   empty split-among selection is refused.
7. Switching between split methods recalculates the shares fresh rather than carrying over values
   entered under the other method.
8. Editing an existing shared-trip expense's payer or split is in scope for this feature, extending
   the same edit capability the app already has for other expense fields.
9. A departed participant (removed or left per feature 019) still appears by name on any expense
   they were previously attributed to, consistent with feature 019's assumption that past
   expenses aren't rewritten.

## Out of Scope

- Percentage-based splitting.
- Computing or displaying running balances/who-owes-whom across the group — that is feature 021.
- Marking any part of a split as settled — that is feature 022.
- Re-attributing a departed participant's past expenses to someone else.
- Concurrent edits to the same expense's attribution from two devices at once.

## Deferred

- Percentage-based splitting — deferred; equal and exact-amount cover the confirmed v2 scope.
