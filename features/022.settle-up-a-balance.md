# Feature 22: Settle Up A Balance

```gherkin
Feature: Settle up a balance
  As a participant
  I want to mark a debt as settled
  So that the balances view reflects that it's been paid outside the app

  Scenario: I settle a debt in full
    Given the balances view shows I owe another participant SGD 20.00
    When I mark that amount as settled
    Then the balance between us is cleared

  Scenario Outline: Either party in a debt can mark it settled
    Given <actor> is part of an outstanding balance between two participants
    When <actor> marks it as settled
    Then the balance is updated for both participants

    Examples:
      | actor                  |
      | the person who owes    |
      | the person who is owed |

  Scenario: I settle part of a debt
    Given I owe another participant SGD 20.00
    When I mark SGD 5.00 of it as settled
    Then the remaining outstanding balance is SGD 15.00

  Scenario: A zero, negative, or non-numeric settle amount is refused
    Given I am settling a balance
    When I enter a zero, negative, or non-numeric amount
    Then I see an error and nothing is settled

  Scenario: Settling more than the outstanding balance is refused
    Given I owe another participant SGD 20.00
    When I try to settle SGD 25.00
    Then I see an error and nothing is settled

  Scenario: A new expense after a partial settlement nets correctly
    Given I have partially settled part of a balance
    When a new expense is recorded that involves the same two participants
    Then the balance reflects both the settlement and the new expense together

  Scenario: Settling a balance with a departed participant
    Given a participant who has left or been removed still has an outstanding balance with me
    When I mark that balance as settled
    Then it is cleared like any other balance

  Scenario: Settling requires confirmation
    Given I am about to mark a balance as settled
    When I trigger the settle action
    Then I am asked to confirm before it takes effect

  Scenario: Settling fails when the server is unreachable
    Given I confirm settling a balance
    When the app cannot reach the server
    Then I see a friendly error message
    And the balance is unchanged

  Scenario: Settling while offline
    Given my device has no internet connection
    When I try to settle a balance
    Then I see a friendly message that this requires connectivity
```

## Assumptions

1. Settling does not move real money; it only records that a debt was paid outside the app (cash,
   another app, etc.), reducing or clearing the tracked balance.
2. Either party to a debt — the person who owes or the person who is owed — can mark it settled
   unilaterally; no confirmation from the other party is required.
3. Partial settlement is supported: a user can settle any amount from just above zero up to the
   full outstanding balance, not only the whole amount at once.
4. A settlement is recorded as an adjustment that combines with the underlying expense-based
   ledger, so later expenses between the same two participants net correctly against it rather
   than being overwritten by it.
5. A balance can still be settled with a participant who has since left or been removed (feature
   019), consistent with feature 021's rule that their balance still counts.
6. Once a balance reaches zero (by settlement or otherwise), it is omitted from the balances view,
   reusing feature 021's existing "zero balance not shown" behavior rather than new display logic.
7. There is no visible history of past settlements in this feature; settling only updates the
   current outstanding balance.
8. Settling requires connectivity, consistent with `OVERVIEW.md` §2's shared-trip connectivity
   requirements, since the underlying balance data lives server-side.

## Out of Scope

- A settlement history or log of who settled what and when.
- Any real payment processing, money transfer, or integration with a payment provider.
- Concurrent settlement of the same balance from both sides at nearly the same time — treated the
  same as feature 020's general concurrent-edit exclusion for v2.
- Undoing or reversing a settlement once confirmed.
- Notifying the other participant that a settlement occurred, outside of the balances view itself.

## Deferred

None.
