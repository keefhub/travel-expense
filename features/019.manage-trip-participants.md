# Feature 19: Manage Trip Participants

```gherkin
Feature: Manage trip participants
  As a creator or participant
  I want to see who's in the trip and leave it, or remove someone else if I'm the creator
  So that the trip's membership stays accurate

  Scenario: I view the trip's participants
    Given I am part of a shared trip with other participants
    When I open the participants screen
    Then I see everyone currently in the trip
    And the creator is visibly labeled as the creator

  Scenario Outline: Only the creator can remove other participants
    Given I am <my role> on a shared trip with another participant
    When I try to remove that other participant
    Then the removal <outcome>

    Examples:
      | my role       | outcome                                 |
      | the creator   | succeeds, and they are no longer listed |
      | a participant | is not available to me                  |

  Scenario: A participant leaves the trip voluntarily
    Given I am a participant (not the creator) on a shared trip
    When I choose to leave the trip
    Then I am no longer listed as a participant

  Scenario: The creator has no option to leave their own trip
    Given I am the creator of a shared trip
    When I view my own participant options
    Then I am not offered a "leave" action for myself

  Scenario: Removing or leaving preserves past expense history
    Given a participant has past expenses recorded on the trip
    When that participant is removed or leaves
    Then their past expenses still show their name
    And the person is no longer listed as a current participant

  Scenario: Removing the correct participant among duplicate names
    Given two participants on the trip share the same display name
    When the creator removes one specific one of them
    Then only that specific participant is removed
    And the other, same-named participant remains

  Scenario: Leaving or removing requires confirmation
    Given I am about to leave the trip or remove another participant
    When I trigger that action
    Then I am asked to confirm before it takes effect

  Scenario: Leaving or removing while offline
    Given my device has no internet connection
    When I try to leave the trip or remove another participant
    Then I see a friendly message that this requires connectivity

  Scenario: Leaving or removing fails when the server is unreachable
    Given I confirm leaving the trip or removing another participant
    When the app cannot reach the server
    Then I see a friendly error message
    And the participant list is unchanged
```

## Assumptions

1. Removing a participant, or a participant leaving, does not rewrite or delete their past
   recorded expenses — the expense keeps the departed participant's name, mirroring the deleted
   custom category precedent in `OVERVIEW.md` §3, assumption 4.
2. Only the trip creator can remove other participants; a non-creator participant has no remove
   action available to them for anyone.
3. Any non-creator participant can leave voluntarily at any time, with no approval required.
4. The creator cannot leave their own trip through this feature — since they own the share link
   (feature 016), the equivalent action for them is deleting/replacing the trip, which is out of
   this feature's scope.
5. A participant is identified for removal by their underlying participant record, not by display
   name, so duplicate names (allowed per feature 017) never cause ambiguity.
6. Leaving and removing both require an explicit confirmation step before taking effect, since
   both are hard to reverse.
7. Leaving or removing a participant requires connectivity, consistent with `OVERVIEW.md` §2's
   shared-trip connectivity requirements.

## Out of Scope

- What a removed or departed participant's own device sees the next time it tries to access the
  trip (e.g. being redirected out) — not addressed by this feature.
- Attributing expenses to participants or computing splits/balances — that is feature 020.
- Re-inviting a removed participant, or any block/ban list preventing them from rejoining via the
  same link.
- Transferring trip ownership to another participant.
- Editing a participant's own display name after joining (carried over from feature 017's scope).

## Deferred

- What happens on a removed/departed participant's own device (do they lose the trip from their
  switcher, see an error, etc.) — deferred because the feature file doesn't define the intended
  behavior and it depends on how feature 018's switcher handles an unavailable trip.
